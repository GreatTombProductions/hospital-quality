5,432 U.S. hospitals — compare quality ratings, mortality outcomes, patient experience, and more. CMS data.

Data from CMS Provider Data — Hospital General Information. CMS star ratings: 1–5 scale based on quality measures across mortality, safety, readmission, patient experience, and timely care. "Not Available" means the hospital didn't have enough data for CMS to calculate a rating. This is an independent tool — not affiliated with any government agency.
