# Hospital Quality Check

Compare 5,432 U.S. hospitals on CMS quality ratings — mortality outcomes, safety, readmission rates, patient experience, and more.

**Live site:** https://GreatTombProductions.github.io/hospital-quality/

## What this does

Search any U.S. hospital to see:
- CMS Overall Star Rating (1–5 stars)
- Mortality outcomes vs national average (death rates)
- Safety outcomes vs national average (complications, infections)
- Readmission outcomes vs national average
- Patient experience and timely care measure counts
- Hospital type, ownership, emergency services, birthing friendly designation

3,182 hospitals have star ratings. 4,498 have emergency departments. 2,263 are designated birthing friendly.

## Data source

CMS Provider Data — Hospital General Information. Updated quarterly by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services.
